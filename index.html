Yes. ResQ Tyres is on the road 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Leeds, Wakefield and Harrogate, and the phone is answered at all times. Ring 07438 562633 or send a WhatsApp.

How much does mobile tyre fitting cost in Leeds?

One tyre fitted in Leeds costs from £90 (tyre from £40 plus mobile fitting from £50). Mobile fitting is from £50 per tyre, depending on where you are. There's no separate call-out fee, and the price is the same day or night. ResQ confirms the exact price with you by phone before any work starts. Booking a planned fitting for all four tyres? You get 30% off the total price, subject to availability.

Does ResQ repair punctures in Leeds, or only replace tyres?

Both. If the tyre can be safely repaired, ResQ repairs it on the spot: puncture repair in Leeds costs £70 to £120, depending on distance and tyre size. If it can't be repaired, we carry new tyres on the van and fit one there and then.

Where in Leeds can ResQ fit the tyre?

Wherever the vehicle is: your driveway, a work car park, the roadside, a motorway hard shoulder or a service station, anywhere in ResQ's area. Do I pay ResQ a deposit or pay online?

No. ResQ takes no deposit for jobs in Leeds or anywhere else, and there's nothing to pay online. You pay when the job's done, by card or cash.

What if I've lost my locking wheel-nut key?

Tell ResQ when you call. Locking wheel nuts stop most fitters, but we carry the specialist tools to remove them safely anywhere in Leeds. Removal is £25–£40 per nut.

Does ResQ do jump starts in Leeds?

Yes. If your battery is flat, ResQ comes out and jump-starts the car at your home, work or the roadside in Leeds, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Do you do vehicle recovery or towing?

No. Despite the name, ResQ Tyres & Recovery fits and repairs tyres and does jump starts. We don't tow or recover vehicles.

Does ResQ fit run-flat or caravan tyres?

No. ResQ doesn't fit run-flat tyres and doesn't work on caravans. If you're not sure what your car has, read us the writing on the tyre's sidewall when you call and we'll tell you.

Does ResQ do wheel alignment?

No, ResQ doesn't offer wheel alignment. We fit and repair tyres, balance wheels and replace valves.
