Common questions

Questions we get asked

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, call us on 07438 562633.

Can you come out to me now?

Yes — we're mobile 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, including bank holidays. Call 07438 562633 or message us on WhatsApp and we'll come to your home, your workplace or the roadside.

Do you repair punctures, or only replace tyres?

Both. If the tyre can be safely repaired, we repair it. If it can't, we carry new tyres on the van and fit one on the spot. Puncture repair starts at £70 and goes up to £120 depending on distance and tyre size.

Which areas do you cover?

Every Leeds LS district, including LS24 Tadcaster; HG1, HG2 and HG3 around Harrogate; and WF1–WF5, WF8, WF10, WF12 and WF13 across Wakefield, Castleford, Pontefract and Dewsbury. If your postcode isn't on that list, call us anyway — we may still be able to reach you.

How much does mobile tyre fitting cost?

It depends on your tyre size. The price guide on this page gives a fitted range, and mobile fitting is included in that price rather than charged as an extra. We confirm the exact price with you by phone before any work starts. Changing all four? Ask about 30% off the set.

Do I pay a deposit or pay online?

No. There's no deposit and nothing to pay online. You pay on completion, by card or cash, once the job's done.

Where can you fit the tyre?

Wherever the vehicle is — your driveway, a work car park, or at the roadside. The van carries brand-new tyres, on-site balancing and valve replacement, so most jobs are finished in a single visit.

What if I've lost my locking wheel-nut key?

Tell us when you call. Locked wheel nuts stop most fitters, but we carry the specialist tools to remove them safely.

Do you do wheel alignment?

No — we don't offer wheel alignment. We fit and repair tyres, balance wheels and replace valves.
