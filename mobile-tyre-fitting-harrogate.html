Which parts of Harrogate do you cover?

All three Harrogate districts — HG1, HG2 and HG3 — so the town centre, Bilton, Starbeck, Oatlands, Pannal, Killinghall, Ripley and the villages out towards Pateley Bridge. Plus LS24 for Tadcaster. Knaresborough and Ripon are just outside, but call and ask.

Can you come out to Harrogate now?

Yes, between 6am and 10pm seven days a week. We come up from Leeds, so tell us where you are when you call 07438 562633 or WhatsApp us, and we'll give you a straight answer on when we can be with you.

Do you repair punctures in Harrogate and Tadcaster?

Yes — if the tyre can be safely repaired, we repair it where the car is. If it can't, we carry new tyres on the van and fit one on the spot. Puncture repair is £70 to £120 depending on distance and tyre size.

Where can you fit the tyre?

Wherever the vehicle is — a driveway in Pannal, a work car park at Hornbeam Park, the Stray, or the hard shoulder of the A61. The van carries brand-new tyres, on-site balancing and valve replacement, so most jobs are finished in one visit.

How much is mobile tyre fitting in Harrogate?

Tyres are from £40 each for 14–17" wheels and from £80 for 18–20", and mobile fitting is from £50 on top depending on where you are — Harrogate and Tadcaster are a longer run from Leeds than the city, so we'll always tell you the fitting price before we set off. Use the price guide for your size. Changing all four? Ask about 30% off the set.

Do I pay a deposit?

No. Nothing to pay online and no deposit. You pay on completion, by card or cash, once the job's done.
