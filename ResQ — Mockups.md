# ResQ — Mockups

Two homepage design directions for the new emergency / mobile-fitting site. Both are **phone-first** and both include a working **tyre-size → price → book** demo (sample prices; reg-lookup stubbed).

## Direction A — Emergency Bold
`Assets/Mockups/Mockup A - Emergency Bold.html`

Dark, high-urgency, roadside-rescue feel. Pulsing call button, big red CTAs, phone unmissable. Leans into the 24/7 emergency identity.

## Direction B — Clean Retail
`Assets/Mockups/Mockup B - Clean Retail.html`

Bright, trustworthy, Hometyre / Black Circles style. Reg + tyre-size lookup front and centre, "3 simple steps", polished and reassuring. Emergency call still always visible. → references [[ResQ — Example Sites]].

## What's real vs placeholder

- **Working:** the price tool (enter reg or pick a size → three sample price tiers), responsive layout, sticky mobile call bar, click-to-call.
- **Placeholder:** prices (simple rim-size formula), reviews, and the logo. "Book" shows a demo prompt.
- **To make real:** wire in the client's **tyre price catalogue**; decide platform for true **reg-lookup** (vehicle-data API) — see [[ResQ — Wix Capability & Costs]].
