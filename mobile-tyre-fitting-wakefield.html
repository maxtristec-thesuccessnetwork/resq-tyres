Do you cover all of Wakefield?

Yes — every district from WF1 to WF13. That's Wakefield itself, Ossett, Horbury, Normanton, Featherstone, Pontefract, Hemsworth, Castleford, Knottingley and Dewsbury. Type your postcode into the checker above if you want to be sure.

Can you come out to me now in Wakefield?

Yes, between 6am and 10pm seven days a week — and Wakefield is one of the areas we're in most. Call 07438 562633 or message us on WhatsApp — for an emergency, ringing is faster than a form.

Do you repair punctures in Wakefield, or only replace tyres?

Both. If the tyre can be safely repaired, we repair it at the roadside or on your drive. If it can't, we carry new tyres on the van and fit one on the spot. Puncture repair is £70 to £120 depending on distance and tyre size.
