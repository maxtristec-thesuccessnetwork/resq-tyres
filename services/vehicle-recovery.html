When recovery is the right call

Recovery is what happens when the problem in front of us cannot safely be sorted where the vehicle is standing. In our line of work that is usually tyre-related: a sidewall that has been destroyed rather than punctured, two or more tyres damaged in the same incident, a kerb strike that has bent or cracked the wheel itself, or a car with no serviceable spare in a size we cannot get hold of at that hour.

It applies outside tyres too — a vehicle that will not start, will not steer properly, or is not sitting right on the road. The test is simple. If we cannot put it back on the road in a condition we would be happy to drive ourselves, it gets recovered instead.

What happens when we get to you

Call or message 07438 562633 and you will speak to someone who is actually working, not a call centre reading from a script. We will ask what has happened, exactly where you are and what you are driving, then head straight out. We are typically with a customer in 15 to 30 minutes.

On arrival we assess the vehicle before deciding anything. If it can be sorted there, we sort it. If it cannot, we explain why, agree with you where the vehicle needs to go, then load it, secure it and get moving. There is no deposit to pay to get us out — you pay on completion, once the work is done and you can see it.

Where we can take your vehicle

Once the vehicle is loaded, it goes where you want it to go. Home, if you would rather deal with it in daylight. Your own garage, or one you trust. A main dealer, if it is under warranty or needs specialist attention. A tyre supplier in the morning, if the size you need is not available overnight.

We have no interest in steering you towards a particular workshop. Our job ends when your vehicle is where you asked for it to be. If you are unsure what makes sense, say so on the phone and we will talk it through with you before we set off.

How this compares to waiting on a breakdown provider

Most breakdown policies work by passing the job on. Your call is logged, the work goes out to whoever happens to be free in the area, and then you wait — usually with a wide time window and very little say in it. That is tolerable on a quiet weekday afternoon. It is a different matter at night on a slip road with children in the back.

We take the call directly, so the person you speak to is the person arranging your recovery. There is no queue between you and the work. Established in 2023 and rated 5.0 on Google from more than 215 reviews, we have built the business on turning up when we said we would.

Good to know

Common questions

Do you always recover, or will you try to fix the vehicle first?

We try to fix it first, every time. Roadside repair or a tyre fitted on the spot is quicker and cheaper for you, so that is where we start. Recovery is what we fall back on when the vehicle genuinely cannot be made safe to drive where it is standing.

How quickly can you get to me?

We are typically with a customer in 15 to 30 minutes across our coverage area. Distance, time of day and traffic all play a part, so we will give you a realistic idea when you call rather than an optimistic one. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Where will you take my vehicle?

Wherever you need it. Home, your usual garage, a dealership, or a tyre supplier for the morning. We do not have a preferred workshop and we will not push you towards one. If you are not sure what is best, mention it on the phone and we will talk through the options.

Do I need to pay anything before you come out?

No deposit is required to get us on the road to you. You pay on completion, once the job is finished. We will be clear with you on the phone about what is involved before we set off, so nothing about the job comes as a surprise when we arrive.

Which areas do you cover for vehicle recovery?

We cover Leeds, Bradford, Wakefield, Dewsbury, Pudsey, Morley, Castleford, Garforth, Pontefract and the surrounding areas, across LS, BD and WF postcodes. If you are near the edge of that and unsure, call 07438 562633 and we will tell you straight away whether we can reach you.
