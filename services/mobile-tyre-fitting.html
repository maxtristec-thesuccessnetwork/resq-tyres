What mobile tyre fitting actually involves

You ring or message us with your registration, your tyre size and where you are. We source the tyre, load it into the van and drive to you. There is no need to get the car to a garage, wait in a reception area, or take an afternoon off work.

The van is a fully equipped Mercedes Sprinter — effectively a garage on wheels. It carries everything needed to remove the old tyre, fit and inflate the new one, balance the wheel properly and torque it back up. We can do this on a driveway, in a car park, in a work car park, or at the side of the road. In most cases you are back on the move within the hour of us arriving.

If the tyre turns out not to be the problem, or the vehicle simply cannot be made safe where it stands, we recover it instead. You are not left to work out a plan B on your own.

Locking wheel nuts, balancing and valves

Two things commonly stop a roadside tyre change: a missing locking wheel nut key, and a wheel that needs more than just a new tyre on it.

If you are replacing all four tyres, there is 30% off, subject to availability of the tyres you need. Worth mentioning when you call if more than one is close to the legal limit.

We replace valves at the same time, which is worth doing on an older wheel rather than putting a new tyre onto a tired valve.

Good to know

Common questions

How quickly can you get to me in Leeds?

We are typically with a customer within 15 to 30 minutes. Actual timing depends on where you are in the coverage area and how busy we are when you call. Whoever answers the phone will give you an honest estimate at the time, so you know whether to wait in the vehicle or somewhere warmer.

Do you fit tyres at night and at weekends?

Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, including weekends and bank holidays. There is no separate out-of-hours service and no different number to ring. Call or WhatsApp 07438 562633 whenever the problem happens, and we will come out to you.

What if I have lost my locking wheel nut key?

That is a common one and it does not stop us. We carry specialist locking wheel nut removal tools in the van and can get the wheel off without the original key. Mention it when you call so we know what we are coming to, and we will bring the right kit with us.

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

No. There is no deposit and no online payment. You pay once the work is finished. For a figure before we set off, use the online estimate or ring us with your registration and tyre size, and we will confirm what we can source and what it costs.

Can you fit tyres at my home by appointment?

Yes. As well as emergency call-outs, we offer planned home fitting Monday to Friday. You book a time, we come to your address, and it costs the same as having the work done in store. It suits people who would rather not lose an afternoon sitting in a waiting room.
