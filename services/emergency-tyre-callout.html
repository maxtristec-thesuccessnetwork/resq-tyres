If you are stranded right now, read this first

Deal with your safety before you deal with the tyre. The wheel can wait.

Once you are somewhere safe, ring us and tell us where you are. Junction numbers, marker posts and what3words all help us find you quickly.

Put your hazard lights on straight away, and your sidelights as well if it is dark or visibility is poor.
On a motorway or dual carriageway, get as far left as you canthe hard shoulder or an emergency refuge area — and stop as far from the running lane as the space allows.
Leave the vehicle through the left-hand doors, away from passing traffic, and get everyone behind the barrier and up the bank if there is one. Keep pets in the car.
Wear something high-visibility if you have it, and do not stand in front of or behind your vehicle.
Do not attempt to change a wheel on the hard shoulder or anywhere close to live traffic. It is simply not worth the risk.
If you cannot get clear of the carriageway, or anything about the situation feels unsafe, stay belted in with your hazards on and ring 999.

What happens after you call

You speak to someone who knows tyres, not a call centre. We will ask what you are driving, your tyre size if you can read it off the sidewall, and exactly where you are. If you would rather send a photo of the wheel or drop us a location pin, WhatsApp the same number.

We then load what your vehicle needs and set off. Our Mercedes Sprinter is a garage on wheels — fitting equipment, balancer, compressor, lighting and a working stock of tyres — so in most cases the job is finished at the roadside in one visit rather than turning into a two-trip saga.

What we can sort out at the roadside

Most call-outs we attend are finished where the vehicle sits. That includes blowouts, punctures, slow flats that have finally given up, kerbed and split sidewalls, and the classic no-spare-in-the-boot situation that catches out so many modern cars.

We fit and balance the new tyre on site, so you are not driving away with a vibration through the steering wheel. We can replace perished or leaking valves at the same time, which is often the real cause of a tyre that keeps going soft.

We also carry specialist locking wheel nut removal tools. If the key has gone missing, or it has rounded off in the socket, that alone does not end the job.

Where we come out to

We cover Leeds and a wide stretch of West Yorkshire around it — Bradford, Wakefield, Dewsbury, Pudsey, Morley, Castleford, Garforth, Pontefract and the surrounding towns and villages. In practice, that means the LS, BD and WF postcodes.

We attend homes, workplaces, supermarket car parks, laybys, retail parks and the motorway and A-road network that runs through the region. Working 24 hours a day, 365 days a year means the middle of the night, Christmas Day and bank holiday weekends are all normal working hours for us.

If it cannot be fixed where you are

Sometimes a roadside fix is not the right answer. The wheel itself may be damaged, there may be more than one tyre gone, or the location may be too exposed to work in safely.

In that case we recover the vehicle instead. You are not left choosing between an unsafe bodge and a long wait for a second company to be arranged. The same phone call covers both outcomes, and we will tell you honestly which one you are looking at before we start.

Good to know

Common questions

Are you really available 24 hours a day?

Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, including nights, weekends and bank holidays. A flat tyre at three in the morning is treated exactly the same as one at three in the afternoon. Ring 07438 562633 whenever it happens and you will get a person, not an answerphone.

How quickly can you get to me?

We are typically with a customer within 15 to 30 minutes across our coverage area. The exact time depends on where you are and what the roads are doing. When you call, we will give you a realistic estimate rather than an optimistic one, so you know whether to wait in a safe spot nearby.

Do you cover motorways and dual carriageways?

Yes, we attend motorway and A-road call-outs throughout the LS, BD and WF postcodes. Get yourself and any passengers behind the barrier first, then call us with your direction of travel and the nearest junction or marker post. If you cannot leave the carriageway safely, ring 999 before you ring us.

Do I have to pay a deposit or book online?

No. There is no deposit and no online payment. You pay on completion, once the work is finished. We think that is only fair when someone is calling in an emergency, and it means you are not committing money to a job before you have met the fitter.

What if I have lost my locking wheel nut key?

That is a common one and it does not usually stop us. We carry specialist locking wheel nut removal tools for keys that have been lost, damaged or rounded off. Mention it when you call so we bring the right kit with us and can get straight on with the job when we arrive.
