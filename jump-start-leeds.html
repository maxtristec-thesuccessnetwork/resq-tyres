Common questions

Jump starts — your questions

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, call us on 07438 562633.

How much is a jump start?

Jump starts are from £50. The exact price depends on where you are, and we'll always confirm it on the phone before we set off. You pay on completion, by card or cash.

Where do you do jump starts?

Across the same area we cover for tyres: every Leeds LS district, Wakefield (WF1–WF13), Harrogate (HG1–HG3), Tadcaster, York and Selby. At home, at work or at the roadside. Not sure? Call and ask.

When can you come out?

Between 6am and 10pm, seven days a week. Call 07438 562633 or WhatsApp us with where you are, and we'll give you a straight answer on when we can be with you. Outside those hours, send a WhatsApp and we'll pick it up first thing.

What should I tell you when I call?

Where the car is, the make and model, and what happened — for example the lights were left on, or it hasn't been driven for a while. It helps us come prepared.

Can you help with a tyre at the same time?

Yes. We're a mobile tyre service first, so if you've also got a flat or damaged tyre, tell us when you call and we'll bring the right tyre on the van.

Do I pay a deposit?

No. Nothing to pay online and no deposit. You pay on completion, once the car is running.
